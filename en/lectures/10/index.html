Lecture 10

The periodic Toda chain: spectral curve and transfer matrix

What this lecture does

Close the open chain of Lecture 9 into a ring. Its Lax matrix becomes a tight-binding Hamiltonian, and Bloch’s theorem puts a variable zzz into its corners. The same eigenvalue problem, as a first-order recursion, gives a 2×22\times22×2 Lax matrix at every site, a discrete zero-curvature equation, and the monodromy matrix of Lecture 8, whose trace t(λ)\transfer(\lambda)t(λ) generates commuting integrals. The ring is Liouville integrable, with tori once its drift is split off, and all its integrals sit in one curve, z+1/z=t(λ)z+1/z=\transfer(\lambda)z+1/z=t(λ): bands, gaps, and two spectral parameters that read the same curve two ways.
