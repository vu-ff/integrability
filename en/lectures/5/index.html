Lecture 5

Action-angle variables in practice

What this lecture does

Lecture 4 proved that the motion of an integrable system lives on tori. This lecture computes on them. We define the action variables, prove that they are well defined and that their derivatives are the periods, and build the conjugate angles from a generating function. Then the oscillator and the pendulum, in both of its regimes, done completely; and for Kepler the radial action and the frequencies — which all coincide, and that is why its orbits close.

5.5 Superintegrability and Laplace–Runge–Lenz

Equal frequencies are the footprint of extra integrals. For Kepler the extra one is the Laplace–Runge–Lenz vector A=p×ℓ−α q∣q∣. A=p\times\ell-\alpha\,\frac{q}{\abs{q}} .
