Lecture 18

The algebraic Bethe ansatz

What this lecture does

The Bethe equations of Lecture 15, derived a second time without ever writing a wavefunction. One entry of the monodromy matrix creates magnons; the RTTRTTRTT relation says how the diagonal entries move past it; acting with the transfer matrix on a product of creation operators gives the wanted term and a set of unwanted ones, and the unwanted ones cancel exactly when the Bethe equations hold. What is left is the eigenvalue of the whole commuting family, and it satisfies Baxter’s T–Q relation: Lecture 19 takes the Bethe equations and those eigenvalues to the thermodynamic limit, Lecture 20 generalises T–Q itself. The same steps, with sinh⁡\sinhsinh for linear functions, solve the XXZ chain.
